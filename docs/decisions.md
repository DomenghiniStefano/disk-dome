Every product and technical decision with its reason, newest first. A change of direction is a new entry,
never an edit of an old one.

| 2026-11-26 | Design-system components are **library-grade** (shadcn / Radix level): full behaviour, every placement and overflow case, accessible, parameterised with token defaults, nothing specific to DiskDome (`docs/design-system.md` § 8.0) | the owner wants components that hold in every case and serve future Slint projects too |

| 2026-11-26 | The scan pool has **eight threads** whatever the cores (`DEFAULT_THREADS`), 4 MB reads; replaces «twice the cores, at most 32» | on the KIOXIA laptop (14 GB `$MFT`) more concurrent reads slow the drive down: read alone 4–8 threads 4.6–5.6 s, 16 in 10 s, 32 in 15–30 s, 64 in 45 s; full scan 7.3–7.6 s with eight against 31–35 s with 32, peak 0.90 GB. On the desktop's Crucial P3 the read rate did not depend on the threads (~640 MB/s) |
| 2026-11-26 | Performance targets restated (`docs/architecture.md` § Performance targets): the scan adds at most 5 s to reading the `$MFT` alone, is at least 3× faster than WizTree on the same PC, peaks ≤ 1.5 GB on 14 million entries and matches ≥ 99% of WizTree's folder totals; replaces «scan ≤ 8 s» | the proof on the KIOXIA laptop (14.1 M entries, 14 GB `$MFT`): reading the `$MFT` alone takes 28–30 s (~470 MB/s), the scan 31–35 s, peak 0.99 GB, 99.8% of folders identical; WizTree 182 s and 2.0 GB on the same run. 8 s would need 1.75 GB/s, never seen on this drive; the «3–4 s» read in `docs/research.md` was computed from the sequential speed, not measured. Owner's request: targets that make sense and can be met |
| 2026-11-26 | Screens talk to Rust only through `export global` APIs, one per area (`ScanApi`, `TreeApi`…), with properties and callbacks; Rust wires each in one adapter module | screens stay pure components the Kit can show with fake data, and the Rust ↔ Slint boundary is in one place per area; globals are not shared between windows, so each window wires its own |
